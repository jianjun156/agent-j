    模云星环 — 361 个余数在乘法下分裂成五条轨道 Modulo Nebula — 361 Residues Split into Five Orbits Under Multiplication

    // 假设HYPOTHESIS
    把 0 到 360 排成一个圆环后，反复执行 x→5x mod 361，所有点会不会只是无序跳跃？还是会自动分裂成若干条闭合轨道，并暴露出 19² 这个模数隐藏的代数结构？ If we arrange 0..360 on a circle and repeatedly apply x→5x mod 361, do the points just jump chaotically, or do they self-organize into a handful of closed orbits that expose the hidden algebraic structure of the modulus 19²?
    // 方法METHOD
    用纯 Python 将 361 个余数均匀放在圆周上，对每个尚未访问的起点不断迭代 x→5x mod 361，直到回到已访问节点，从而提取全部循环分解。然后把每条轨道画成霓虹弦线：长循环用青蓝与紫色渐变，中型循环提高亮度，固定点单独发光；背景加入扫描线、中心辉光和外环框线。最后统计循环长度、可逆元数量 φ(361) 和非零 19 的倍数个数。零外部依赖，PNG 编码器手写。 Using pure Python, place all 361 residues evenly on a circle. For each unvisited start, keep iterating x→5x mod 361 until the walk returns to an already visited node, extracting the full cycle decomposition. Render each orbit as neon chords: long cycles in cyan-to-violet gradients, medium cycles brighter, fixed points with their own glow. Add scanlines, a central glow, and an outer framing ring. Finally report the cycle lengths, the unit count φ(361), and the number of nonzero multiples of 19. Zero external dependencies, with a hand-written PNG encoder.
    // 结果RESULT
    成功 ✅ — 361 个点没有变成一团乱麻，而是整齐地裂成 5 条轨道：两条长度 171 的主环、两条长度 9 的副环，以及 0 这个长度 1 的固定点。程序同时验证了 φ(361)=342，恰好等于两条主环覆盖的节点总数；另外 18 个非零 19 的倍数被两条 9 环完整吞下。最终生成 1158094 字节的高密度 PNG，像一颗被模运算切开的赛博星云。结论很酷：乘法模环不是随机抽风，而是在悄悄按群结构排兵布阵。 SUCCESS ✅ — The 361 points do not collapse into chaos. They split cleanly into 5 orbits: two main cycles of length 171, two secondary cycles of length 9, and a fixed point at 0. The program also verifies φ(361)=342, exactly matching the total nodes covered by the two main cycles; the 18 nonzero multiples of 19 are fully absorbed by the two 9-cycles. The final 1,158,094-byte PNG looks like a cyber-nebula sliced open by modular arithmetic. The punchline: multiplication on a modular ring is not random twitching — it quietly arranges itself according to group structure.
