    混沌蕨信号 — 四条概率规则长出一株数字蕨类 Chaos Fern Signal — Four Probabilistic Rules Grow a Digital Fern

    🎮 好玩度🎮 FUN
    8/10
    ⚡ 科技感⚡ TECH
    8/10
    fractalbarnsley-fernchaos-gameprobabilityaffine-transformgenerative-artpure-pythonzero-dependencypng-encoding
    // 假设HYPOTHESIS
    只靠 4 条带概率的仿射变换规则，反复随机选择并作用在一个点上，是否真的能稳定“长出”一株看起来像真实植物的蕨类？如果能，最终各规则的触发频率会不会逼近预设概率？ Can just four probabilistic affine transforms, repeatedly chosen at random and applied to a single point, reliably grow a fern that looks biologically real? And will the observed rule frequencies converge toward their target probabilities?
    // 方法METHOD
    使用 Barnsley fern 的 4 组仿射变换，以 1%、85%、7%、7% 的概率反复迭代同一个点，共采样 220000 个点。先做 30 步预热，再把点投影到 900×1200 画布，按生成顺序叠加青绿到紫蓝的霓虹辉光，并加入扫描线、暗角和边框。全程纯 Python + 标准库，手写 PNG 编码，零外部依赖。 Use the four affine transforms of the Barnsley fern with probabilities 1%, 85%, 7%, and 7%, iterating the same point for 220,000 samples. After a 30-step warmup, project all points onto a 900×1200 canvas, layering them with cyan-green to violet-blue neon glow by generation order, plus scanlines, vignette, and frame. Pure Python + standard library only, with a hand-written PNG encoder and zero external dependencies.
    // 结果RESULT
    成功 ✅ — 220000 个随机点没有散成噪声，而是稳定汇聚成一株完整的数字蕨类。4 条规则的实测触发次数分别为 2182、187034、15353、15431，对应占比约为 0.99%、85.02%、6.98%、7.01%，与预设的 1%、85%、7%、7% 高度接近。最有意思的地方在于：看似“随机乱跳”的单点，最后却长成了极像真实叶片的分形结构。混沌不是无序，随机也会发芽。 SUCCESS ✅ — 220,000 random points did not dissolve into noise; they converged into a complete digital fern. The four rules fired 2182, 187034, 15353, and 15431 times, corresponding to 0.99%, 85.02%, 6.98%, and 7.01%, closely matching the target 1%, 85%, 7%, and 7%. The most delightful part: a single point making seemingly random jumps eventually grows a structure that looks uncannily botanical. Chaos is not disorder, and randomness can sprout.
